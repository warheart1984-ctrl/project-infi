## 1. Mission

To establish the immutable principles governing all learning, competency development, evidence creation, and stewardship within the Constitutional Cyber & AI Learning Framework (CCALF).

## 2. Scope

These principles apply to all CCALF specifications, domains, implementations, learners, organizations, and governance bodies.

## 3. Constitutional Principles

### 050.1 Evidence Before Claims

All assertions of knowledge, competency, or stewardship must be supported by verifiable evidence.

No domain, learner, or curriculum may assert maturity without demonstrable artifacts.

### 050.2 Separation of Concerns

Learning architecture, governance, evidence, conformance, and domain specifications must remain independent modules with defined interfaces.

No module may assume authority over another without explicit constitutional linkage.

### 050.3 Traceability

Every learning artifact, decision, maturity assignment, and governance action must be traceable to its origin, rationale, and evidence.

### 050.4 Reproducibility

Competency demonstrations must be reproducible by independent reviewers following documented procedures.

### 050.5 Reviewability

All CCALF artifacts must be open to structured review, commentary, and revision under the governance specification.

### 050.6 Stewardship

Learners and organizations must demonstrate ethical, lawful, and responsible practice aligned with CCALF principles.

### 050.7 Interoperability

Domains, evidence types, and conformance processes must be interoperable across organizations, technologies, and learning environments.

## 4. Constitutional Authority

These principles supersede all other CCALF specifications.

Any specification or domain in conflict with CCALF-050 is invalid until revised.

## Relationship to host product charters

Within Mandala Rendering Software (MRS), CCALF-050 governs **learning and competency stewardship**. MRS `AGENTS.md`, `constitution/CHARTER.md`, and machine charter under `engine/constitution/` remain the binding constitutional authority for agents and runtime in that repository. CCALF does not rewrite 4DCE/4DRS.
