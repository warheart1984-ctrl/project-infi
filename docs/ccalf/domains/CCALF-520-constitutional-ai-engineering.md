## 1. Domain Mission

To define the knowledge, architectures, evidence, and stewardship required for constitutional AI engineering — the design, implementation, and governance of AI systems that operate under constitutional constraints.

## 2. Domain Layers

### 520.1 Foundations

- Constitutional computing
- Governed intelligence
- AI safety primitives
- Authority–Evidence–Decision chains

### 520.2 Standards

- CIEMS
- NIST AI RMF
- ISO/IEC AI standards
- Constitutional AI governance models

### 520.3 Reference Architectures

- Constitutional Execution Environment (COE)
- Constitutional Runtime (JCR)
- Constitutional Evidence Ledger
- Intent Lifecycle Contract (ILC)
- Constitutional Inference Contract (CIC)
- Constitutional Continuity Contract (CCC)

### 520.4 Implementations

- Governed model pipelines
- Evidence-backed inference systems
- Constitutional decision engines
- Replay and audit mechanisms

### 520.5 Evidence

- Architecture documents
- Constitutional chain mappings

- Stewardship reflections
- Governance interactions
